perform independent audit/review. Choose model and supported effort by task
difficulty, required local tools, source access, observed capacity and expected
usefulness. Use lower effort for straightforward work, high for bounded
implementation/review, and xhigh or a stronger model for difficult integration
when justified. Record a concrete reason before expensive Pro/Astra escalation.
Provider failures do not justify escalation or duplicate generation.

The pool to qualify includes ChatGPT CDP, Perplexity CDP, Antigravity, Cursor,
local z.ai GLM, and supported local workers. This is a routing pool, not a claim
that every route is authenticated, entitled or ready. Perplexity may use one
suitable verified model; five-model participation is not mandatory. Do not
assume Max entitlement or treat Best as verified identity. Claude CLI Opus
5.5/Fable is optional and reserved for sparse critical decisions; verify the
installed catalog instead of treating those names as capability proof.

A local implementor needs verified edit-capable access to the exact owned root.
A browser model can author concrete code/patches; if its adapter cannot access
the local tree, assign local application and real validation separately. Local
models may author substantive behavior and tests; no mandatory cloud-return loop
or prior cloud failure is required. Attribute author, applier, material local
edits and reviewer honestly. The designated conductor owns only the assigned package and stages. Additional
evidence does not transfer another owner's implementation/review/CI/landing;
retain explicit transfer evidence before adopting them.

## Capacity and accounts

Read non-generative provider quota/status before a job and after its terminal
result where supported. Retain observation time, nonsecret profile alias, model,
window, remaining capacity and reset time. Missing fields stay UNKNOWN; local
receipt counters, tokens, elapsed time and HTTP 429 do not establish subscription
capacity. With QUOTA_5H_UNKNOWN, start at most one bounded job on that provider
before reevaluating; do not generate solely to test quota.

Notify the operator at a reported capacity block or low capacity, including a
verified remaining fraction at or below 20%, and checkpoint the task. Avoid a
new long job until capacity is resolved. Antigravity's five operator accounts
are potential capacity, not five proven concurrent lanes. Account switching is
operator-owned; no automatic rotation, credential inspection, quota reset or
permission bypass is authorized. A rotation proposal is not implementation or
approval of it. After an operator switch, reread model/account availability,
quota if exposed, and the existing task's terminal state before remaining work.
Another qualified provider may continue an independent assignment meanwhile.

It needs an explicit reason, read-only tool capability and empty implementation
write-set. It is unavailable for implementation, integration, tests, monitoring,
routine research/readback and routine review, including native spawn, Sidecar,
CLI and browser routes. Each leaf has explicit model and supported effort; do
not inherit an Astra parent or select it through default, Pro, Best, alias or
fallback. Actual identity is read back from the qualified adapter. The current
host's policy text is CONTEXT_ONLY unless its entrypoint enforces this before
submit; do not claim unattended qualification from instructions alone.

spawn a recovery process or call thread/resume; it is not pure readback by name.
Any such side effect needs current owner/window/cap/resource admission, including
after host restart. Keep a separate pure persisted-output projection when needed.

installed target, enforced dispatch and fresh-session behavior have separate
evidence; install shared rules only after qualified quiescence and migration.
